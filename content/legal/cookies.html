This website doesn't use cookies. It doesn't use analytics, advertising or any other tracking either, so there's nothing to accept or turn off, and no cookie banner.

What this website does and doesn't do

  • It doesn't set cookies, and it doesn't store anything on your device (no local storage or similar).
  • It doesn't load anything from other companies' websites: its fonts, pictures and code are served from the website itself.
  • The booking calendar and the chat keep what you type only while the page is open.

Other websites

If you follow a link to another website (for example WhatsApp, or our booking system's pages), that website's own cookie policy applies there.

If this changes

If we ever add cookies that aren't strictly necessary, such as analytics, we will update this page and ask for your consent before setting them.
