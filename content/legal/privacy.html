What we collect, and when

When you browse this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or any other tracking, and it doesn't store anything on your device. Its fonts and pictures are served from the website itself, so your browser doesn't contact Google or other companies when you visit. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

When you book an appointment

We collect your name, phone number, email address, the treatment, date and time you choose, the doctor, whether it is in clinic or a call-out (and, for a call-out, the address), and anything you choose to tell us in the notes. Online bookings are handled by our booking system, provided by {{bookingProvider}}, which stores them on our behalf.

When you use "Speak to a doctor" (our chat)

The chat asks what you're looking for, a little about what's been going on, any medical conditions, allergies or medicines, your name, phone number, (if you choose to give it) your email address, and a good time to call. Some of this is information about your health. We only use it if you give your explicit consent in the chat, and only so that one of our doctors can review it and call you for a telephone consultation.

When you contact us

If you call, email or message us, we keep what you send us and our replies. If you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp (part of Meta) also processes your messages under its own privacy policy.

When you have a consultation or treatment

Our doctors take a medical history and keep clinical records of your consultation and treatment, as every doctor must.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To arrange and provide your consultation and treatment, and to contact you about your appointment: because it is necessary for our contract with you, or to take the steps you ask for before one. For health information, because it is needed to provide health care under the responsibility of a doctor.
  • To answer an enquiry made through the chat: with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time (it doesn't affect what we did before you withdrew it).
  • To keep clinical and financial records: because the law and our doctors' professional obligations require it.
  • To keep our services safe and improve them: in our legitimate interests, which never override your rights.

We never sell your information, and we only send you marketing if you have asked us to.

Who we share it with

  • Our doctors and clinic team, who need it to look after you.
  • Companies that provide services to us, such as our booking system, email and website hosting, which may only use it on our instructions.
  • Your GP or another health professional, only with your agreement, or where the law allows it to protect you or others in an emergency.
  • Regulators, the police or courts, when the law requires us to.

If any of your information is stored outside the UK, we make sure it is protected to the standard UK law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Clinical records: for as long as professional guidance requires (for adults, usually at least 8 years after your last treatment).
  • Bookings, payments and accounts: for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting (usually 6 years).
  • Enquiries that don't lead to an appointment: [[how long enquiries are kept, e.g. 12 months]], then deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to have it given to you or another provider in a usable form, and to withdraw your consent at any time. There's no charge, and we'll reply within one month.

Asking us to delete your information

Email {{email}} or call {{phone}} and tell us your name and how we can recognise your records (for example, the phone number or email you used). We'll confirm who you are, then delete your information and tell you when it's done. The one exception is information the law requires us to keep, such as clinical records; if that applies, we'll tell you what we have to keep, why and for how long, and we'll delete it once that time is up.

Age

Our treatments are for people aged {{minimumAge}} and over, and booking and the chat ask you to confirm your age. We don't knowingly collect information about anyone younger. If you think someone under {{minimumAge}} has given us their details, please contact us and we will delete them.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first and we'll try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
