Your legal right to cancel

If you book online or by phone, rather than in person at the clinic, the law (the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013) gives you 14 days from booking to cancel without giving a reason, and we'll refund anything you've paid within 14 days of you telling us. If you ask us to give you the treatment within those 14 days, you can still cancel before it, but once the treatment has been given you can't cancel it. If you cancel part-way, we may charge for what has already been provided.
