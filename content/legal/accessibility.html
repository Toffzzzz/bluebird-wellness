We want everyone to be able to use this website, whatever device or assistive technology you use.

What we do

  • We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.
  • Every page, the booking calendar and the chat can be used with a keyboard alone, with visible focus. Pop-ups keep the focus inside them, and Escape closes them.
  • Pictures and animations have text descriptions, and decorative ones are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and buttons meet contrast guidelines, and pages still work when zoomed to 200% or on a small screen.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, nothing moves with the scroll or by itself: the treatments' pictures are shown finished.
  • We test every change with automated checks (text alternatives, names for every link and button, colour contrast, headings) and by going through every page with the keyboard.
