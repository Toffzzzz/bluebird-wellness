If something hasn't gone as it should, we want to know, so we can put it right and learn from it. Making a complaint won't affect your care.

How to complain

You can complain to us by phone on {{phone}}, by email to {{email}}, or in person at the clinic. If you'd like someone to complain on your behalf, such as a relative, please tell us so we can check you're happy for us to discuss your care with them. It helps to complain as soon as you can, ideally within [[how long, e.g. 12 months of what happened or of you realising]].

What happens next

  • We'll acknowledge your complaint within [[e.g. 3 working days]].
  • [[Who looks into complaints, e.g. one of our doctors who wasn't involved]] will look into what happened, and may contact you to understand it fully.
  • We'll send you a full reply within [[e.g. 20 working days]]. If it will take longer, we'll tell you why and when to expect it.
  • Our reply will explain what we found, apologise where we got things wrong, and say what we'll do about it.

If you're still unhappy

[[If the clinic subscribes to the Independent Sector Complaints Adjudication Service (ISCAS): you can ask for an internal review, and then for independent adjudication by ISCAS (iscas.cedr.com). If not: how to ask for a review by the clinic's senior doctor.]]

The Care Quality Commission, which regulates health services in England, doesn't generally investigate individual complaints about private care, but it does want to hear about your experience, and may use what you tell it in its inspections: cqc.org.uk/give-feedback-on-care.

If you're concerned about a doctor's fitness to practise, you can raise it with the General Medical Council: gmc-uk.org/concerns.

If your complaint is about how we've handled your personal information, see our privacy policy, which explains how to contact the Information Commissioner's Office.

How we use complaints

We keep a record of every complaint and what we did about it, review them regularly, and use them to improve our care. We'll keep your complaint separate from your medical records.
