Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. Every drip starts with a consultation with one of our doctors, Dr Nema or Dr Mahdi, who will ask about your health, any medicines you take and why you're interested in a drip. The doctor will only go ahead if it's suitable and safe for you, and may advise something else instead, such as seeing your GP.

Do I need a blood test?

Some treatments, such as the Iron Infusion, need a blood test first. The doctor will tell you if yours does. [[How blood tests are arranged and what they cost]].

Is it safe?

Like any treatment given into a vein, a drip carries some risks, such as bruising, discomfort or, rarely, infection or an allergic reaction. The doctor will explain the risks of your treatment before you decide, and [[what monitoring and aftercare the clinic provides]].

What if I feel unwell afterwards?

[[Who to contact after a treatment, and when]]. If you feel very unwell, call NHS 111, or 999 in an emergency.

What does it cost, and how do I pay?

Each treatment's price is on its page. "From" prices depend on the dose the doctor recommends. [[How to pay, e.g. card at the time of treatment]]. See our cancellations and refunds policy for changing or cancelling an appointment.

Can I use the chat or WhatsApp in an emergency?

No. They aren't for emergencies. If you need help now, call 999, or NHS 111 for urgent medical advice.
